## The one-paragraph thesis

CMMC 2.0 is the Pentagon's cybersecurity mandate, phasing into DoD contracts
since late 2025. Every one of ~80,000 small defense suppliers must
self-assess against NIST SP 800-171 controls, produce a System Security Plan
(SSP) + POA&M, and post a score to SPRS — or become **ineligible to bid**.
Their current options: $30–60K consultants or spreadsheet hell. A guided
"TurboTax for CMMC" at $99–299/mo — plain-English questions in, required
documents and an SPRS-ready score out — is a screaming gap at exactly the
price tier and buyer IR already owns. Compliance is the rare product with
government-mandated demand and a deadline.

## Product shape (build in this order)

1. **Level 1 wizard (MVP, ~2–3 weeks of sessions).** CMMC Level 1 is pure
   self-attestation: 17 practices (FAR 52.204-21). Guided plain-English
   questionnaire → gap list → generated policy documents → attestation
   record. Pure software, no assessor needed, immediate value.
2. **Level 2 prep (the real revenue).** 110 NIST 800-171 controls, SPRS
   self-scoring (-203 to +110 methodology), SSP + POA&M generation, evidence
   checklist, Claude-drafted policy language from the user's answers,
   quarterly re-assessment reminders (compliance never ends = churn armor).
3. **Assessor referral network.** Level 2 for critical programs needs a
   C3PAO third-party assessment — we don't certify, we make companies
   *audit-ready* and refer out for a fee. Partner leverage, zero liability
   for the certification itself.
4. **Supply-chain dashboard (enterprise, the $100K+/yr tier).** Primes must
   flow CMMC down to subs — sell primes visibility into their supplier
   base's readiness. This is where it stops being an SMB tool and starts
   being infrastructure.

## Honest risks (carry these into the build)

1. **Regulatory drift.** CMMC rules have shifted for years and will again.
   Mitigation: build the control library as data, not code — rule changes
   become content updates. Re-verify current CMMC/NIST revisions at build
   time; do NOT trust this doc's specifics blindly.
2. **Liability.** We are not a C3PAO and never claim to certify. Every
   output labeled "self-assessment support," attorney-reviewed ToS before
   first sale. The honesty-first brand is load-bearing here.
3. **Incumbents waking up.** Vendors like Summit 7 / PreVeil own the
   upmarket; the gap is self-serve SMB. If someone credible ships cheap
   self-serve first, evaluate partnering instead of competing.
4. **Focus.** The gate exists because this module is exactly the kind of
   shiny thing that kills launches. The gate is the plan.
