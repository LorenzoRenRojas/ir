# Year One — The Golden Goose Plan (Aug 2026 → Aug 2027)

The operating thesis: **Knowledge · Capital · Execution.** Knowledge is the
data and relationships nobody can copy. Capital is staying alive and
reinvesting every dollar with a hypothesis. Execution is the weekly rhythm
that never breaks. Companion to `LAUNCH_SPRINT.md` (the first 9 weeks),
`GROWTH_PLAN.md` (strategy), `COMPETITION.md` (the battlefield).

**Honesty first:** "millions" is a revenue destination, not a Year One
outcome. Year One's job is to build the machine that gets there — prove
people pay, prove they stay, prove a channel compounds. Hit this plan and
the trajectory reads ~$30K MRR (≈$360K ARR run rate) by Aug 2027, with $1M+
ARR in sight for 2028. GovWin doesn't die in Year One; Year One is where we
dig the tunnel under their castle.

## The three pillars, operationalized

**KNOWLEDGE (the un-stealable assets — feed them weekly)**
1. ContractArchive: every notice now accumulates forever (shipped Jul 14).
2. Win/loss outcome data: every user pipeline decision trains the engine.
   Never sell it, never expose it raw, build every "v2" on it.
3. Advisor relationships: every counselor conversation is proprietary
   distribution AND market research. Log what they say.
4. Fiscal/policy calendar awareness folded into every build session
   (FY events, SBA rules, competitor moves → COMPETITION.md stays current).

**CAPITAL (stay alive, reinvest with hypotheses)**
- Pre-revenue burn ceiling: $150/mo. Post-revenue: reinvest 100% — 40%
  growth experiments (each with a kill criterion), 30% product COGS
  (Claude API), 20% cash buffer, 10% brand/events.
- Free money first: cloud/AI credits, pitch comps, SBIR — never equity in
  Year One. VC only ever from traction, probably never.
- Founder liquidity: fixed 3-month buffer, then scheduled monthly owner's
  draw (40% draw / 30% tax reserve / 30% reinvest). CPA at ~$40–50K profit
  for S-corp election. Florida = no state income tax.

**EXECUTION (the rhythm that never breaks)**
- Mon metrics (20 min) → one priority per week. Tue–Thu: 3–5 customer
  conversations. Wed: data post (one tap). Fri: build session, ship, push.
- Every user win logged and asked for a testimonial at the value moment.
- The founder does distribution; AI sessions do code. Never reversed.

## What we do NOT do in Year One

No SLED before gate. No SOC 2 audit before revenue supports it. No paid ads
beyond capped experiments. No enterprise sales motion. No second product.
No equity raised from weakness. No feature built that doesn't serve
find→foresee→track→draft→send or a channel.
