# Launch Sprint — July 28 → September 30, 2026

The 9-week plan from public launch to first real revenue. Companion to
`GROWTH_PLAN.md` (overall strategy) and `COMPETITION.md` (positioning).
Where this doc and GROWTH_PLAN disagree on numbers, this doc is current —
it supersedes GROWTH_PLAN's earlier "25 paying by Sept 30" target.

---

## The goal

**30 paid users by September 30.** That's it. Every hour between launch and
Q3-end gets judged against that number.

The math that gets there: **~5–7 warm demos per week** for 9 weeks ≈ 45–60
demos. At a ~50% close rate on genuinely warm, advisor-referred small
contractors, that's 25–30 paying — no cold outbound, no ads, no miracles
required. The whole sprint is really one discipline: keep the demo calendar
full every single week.

## Channel #1: APEX / PTAC advisors

APEX Accelerator (formerly PTAC) counselors advise thousands of exactly-ICP
small contractors, for free, with **no tool budget of their own**. One
counselor who trusts IR is a recurring stream of warm demos forever.

- **Give every counselor a free account.** No trial clock, no strings. They
  demo IR in their own counseling sessions; their clients arrive pre-sold.
- Lead with the Pipeline Audit (see GROWTH_PLAN): "give me a client's UEI,
  I'll show you their next 18 months" — a deliverable, not a pitch.
- Start with Florida offices (founder's home turf), expand by referral.
- Positioning note per COMPETITION.md: with HigherGov absorbed into
  Procurement Sciences (May 2026), IR is now honestly **the independent tool
  for the small shop** — advisors care about that; say it.

## Founding-member pricing (pending founder approval)

**Recommendation: $49/mo, locked for life, capped at 30 seats.**

- **Why $49:** below every credible alternative's entry point, low enough
  that a solo contractor approves it without deliberating, high enough to
  prove real willingness to pay. This is a founding-member offer, not the
  price — list pricing ($79–499) stands for seat 31 onward.
- **Why locked for life:** the grandfathering promise is the urgency and the
  loyalty program in one. Tell them explicitly, publicly, at the demo.
- **Why 30 seats:** matches the goal exactly — when the founding tier sells
  out, the sprint is won. Scarcity that's true.
- **Status: NOT approved yet.** Founder decision needed before the first
  demo. Do not publish or quote it until approved.

## The marketing hook: FY2026's compressed year-end

This fiscal year is a gift and it expires October 1:

- The **42-day government shutdown** froze the start of FY2026, and full-year
  appropriations weren't completed until **February 2026** — agencies got
  their money months late but still lose it September 30.
- A full year of obligations is being squeezed into ~7 months, pointing to
  the **biggest September obligation surge in years**. Q4 is always the
  federal spending spree; this one is compressed and bigger.
- **Every piece of launch content hangs on this:** "the money is moving
  NOW — here's what's open in your NAICS this month" beats any feature pitch.
  Weekly data posts (from ContractCache + Recompete Radar) count down to
  Sept 30 with live dollar figures. Demos open with it. The urgency is real
  and verifiable, which is exactly the IR brand voice.

## The compounding channel: comparison-SEO pages

Demos get the first 30; search gets the next 300. Ship programmatic
comparison pages — "IR vs HigherGov", "IR vs GovTribe", "GovWin IQ
alternatives for small business", "HigherGov alternatives" (that query is
about to spike post-acquisition) — in the same honest, numbers-first voice as
COMPETITION.md. Buyers searching comparisons are bottom-of-funnel; these
pages sell while the founder sleeps and compound while paid channels don't.
Marketing-claims policy applies: price-tier and integration claims, never
"only platform with X".

## Critical path: the money rails (deadline: mid-August)

Nothing above matters if we can't charge. Sequential, each step blocks the
next, founder-action items:

1. **LLC** — Florida/Sunbiz filing (in progress, ~$125)
2. **EIN** — IRS, free, same-day online once the LLC exists
3. **Business bank account** — needs the EIN
4. **Stripe** — under the LLC, needs the bank account (resolves the personal-
   account verification issue; fallback remains Paddle/Lemon Squeezy)

**All four done by mid-August** — that leaves ~6 weeks of the sprint where
demos can end in a paid conversion instead of an IOU.
